[Lectio1]
Lesson from the book of Ecclesiasticus
!Sir 4:1-4
Son, defraud not the poor of alms, and turn not away thine eyes from the poor. Despise not the hungry soul: and provoke not the poor in his want. Afflict not the heart of the needy, and defer not to give to him that is in distress. Reject not the petition of the afflicted: and turn not away thy face from the needy.

[Lectio2]
!Sir 4:5-7
Turn not away thine eyes from the poor for anger: and leave not them that seek after thee to curse thee behind thy back. For the prayer of him that curseth thee in the bitterness of his soul shall be heard: for He that made him will hear him. Make thyself affable to the congregation of the poor, and humble thy soul to the priest, and bow thy head to a great man.

[Lectio3]
!Sir 4:8-11
Bow down thy ear cheerfully to the poor, and pay what thou owest, and answer him peaceably in meekness. Deliver him that suffereth wrong out of the hand of the proud: and be not faint-hearted in thy soul. In judging be merciful to the fatherless as a father, and as a husband to their mother: and thou shalt be as the obedient son of the Most High, and He will have mercy on thee more than a mother.
