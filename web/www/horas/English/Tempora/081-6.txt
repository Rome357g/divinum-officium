Lesson from the book of Proverbs
!Prov 16:1-5
It is the part of man to prepare the soul, and of the Lord to govern the tongue. All the ways of a man are open to His eyes: the Lord is the weigher of spirits. Reveal thy works to the Lord, and thy thoughts shall be directed. The Lord hath made all things for Himself: the wicked also for the evil day. Every proud man is an abomination to the Lord: though hand should be joined to hand, he is not innocent.

[Lectio2]
!Prov 16:5-9
The beginning of a good way is to do justice: and this is more acceptable with God than to offer sacrifices. By mercy and truth iniquity is redeemed: and by the fear of the Lord men depart from evil. When the ways of man shall please the Lord, He will convert even his enemies to peace. Better is a little with justice, than great revenues with iniquity. The heart of man disposeth his way: but the Lord must direct his steps.

[Lectio3]
!Prov 16:10-15
Divination is in the lips of the king, his mouth shall not err in judgment. Weight and balance are judgments of the Lord: and His work is all the weights of the bag. They that act wickedly are abominable to the king: for the throne is established by justice. Just lips are the delight of kings: he that speaketh right things shall be loved. The wrath of a king is as messengers of death: and a wise man will pacify it. In the cheerfulness of the king’s countenance is life: and his clemency is like the latter rain.
