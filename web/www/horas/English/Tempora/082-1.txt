[Lectio1]
Lesson from the book of Ecclesiastes
!Eccl 2:1-4
I said in my heart: I will go and abound with delights, and enjoy good things; and I saw that this also was vanity. Laughter I counted error, and to mirth I said: Why art thou vainly deceived? I thought in my heart to withdraw my flesh from wine, that I might transfer my mind unto wisdom, and might avoid folly, until I might see what was useful for the children of men, which they ought to do under the sun all the days of their life. I magnified my works, I built me houses, and planted vineyards.

[Lectio2]
!Eccl 2:7-9
I possessed servants and handmaids, and had a great family, herds also, and great flocks of sheep, above all that were before me in Jerusalem; I heaped together for myself silver and gold, and the substance of kings and provinces; I made me singing men and singing women, and the delights of the children of men, cups and vessels to serve to pour out wine; and I surpassed in riches all that were before me in Jerusalem: my wisdom also remained with me.

[Lectio3]
!Eccl 2:10-11
And all that my eyes desired, I refused them not: and I withheld not my heart from enjoying every pleasure, and delighting itself in the things which I had prepared; and I esteemed this my portion, if I should make use of my labour. And when I turned myself to all the works which my hands had done, and to the labours wherein I had laboured in vain, I saw in all things vanity and affliction of mind, and that nothing was lasting under the sun.
