Homily of Saint Augustine the Bishop, Bishop of Hippo
!Book 2 of Questions on the Gospels, chapter 40
Concerning the ten lepers, whom the Lord so cleansed when He said: "Go, show yourselves to the priests.” It may be asked: why did the Lord send them unto the Priests, that, as they went, they might be cleansed? For of none of those [classes of people] to whom He bestowed these bodily benefits [cures] is He found to have sent any to the Priests, except the lepers. For He had also cleansed that other from leprosy, to whom He said: Go, show thyself to the priests, and offer for thyself the sacrifice which Moses commanded, for a testimony unto them." (Luke v. 14, Lev. xiv. seq.) We ask then, of what leprosy was a type, whereof they that were ridded were called, not “healed,” but “cleansed.” It is indeed a defect of colour [in the skin], not of health, or [immediately] of the integrity of the senses and of the members [at first].

[Lectio8]
Lepers therefore can not unreasonably be understood as those who, not having the knowledge of the true faith, profess various doctrines of error. For they do not hide their ignorance; but they bring it forth into the light as the highest skill, and display it by the boastfulness of speech. Moreover there is no false doctrine which doth not mingle some true things therewith. True things therefore mingled in disorder with false, appearing in one disputation or narration of a man, as in the colour of one body, signify leprosy, as it were varying and staining with true and false [colours in the skin].

[Lectio9]
But these are so to be avoided by the Church that, if it can be done, being removed farther off, they may with a great cry call upon Christ; as these ten stood afar off, and lifted up their voice, saying: Jesus, Master, have mercy on us. For also that they call Him Master, by which name I know not whether anyone hath called upon the Lord for a bodily cure; I think sufficiently signifies that leprosy is false doctrine, which a good Master wipeth away.
