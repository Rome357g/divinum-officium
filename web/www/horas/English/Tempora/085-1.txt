[Lectio1]
Lesson from the book of Ecclesiasticus
!Sir 7:1-5
Do no evils, and no evils shall lay hold of thee: depart from the unjust, and evils shall depart from thee. My son, sow not evils in the furrows of injustice, and thou shalt not reap them sevenfold. Seek not of the Lord a leadership, nor of the king the seat of honour. Justify not thyself before God, for He is the knower of the heart: and in the presence of the king desire not to appear wise.  

[Lectio2]
!Sir 7:6-10
Seek not to be made a judge, unless thou have strength enough to root out iniquities; lest perhaps thou fear the face of the powerful, and put a stumbling-block in the way of thy equity. Offend not against the multitude of a city, neither cast thyself in among the people: nor bind thyself with a double sin, for thou shalt not be free even in one. Be not of a small heart in thy soul: despise not to pray and to give alms.  

[Lectio3]
!Sir 7:11-15
Say not: God will have respect to the multitude of my gifts, and when I offer to the most high God, He will accept my offerings. Laugh no man to scorn in the bitterness of his soul: for there is one that humbleth and exalteth, God who beholdeth all. Devise not a lie against thy brother, neither do the like to thy friend. Be not willing to make any manner of lie: for the custom thereof is not good. Be not full of words in a multitude of priests, and repeat not the word in thy prayer.
