[Lectio1]
Lesson from the book of Ecclesiasticus
!Sir 32:1-5
Have they made thee ruler? Be not lifted up: be among them as one of themselves. Take care of them, and so sit down, and when thou hast discharged all thy office, take thy place: that thou mayest rejoice for them, and receive a crown as an ornament of grace, and get the dignity of the contribution. Speak, thou that art elder: for it becometh thee to speak the first word with careful knowledge, and hinder not music.  

[Lectio2]
!Sir 32:6-11
Where there is no hearing, pour not out words, and be not lifted up out of season with thy wisdom. A concert of music in a banquet of wine is as a signet of carbuncle set in gold. As a signet of an emerald in a work of gold: so is the melody of music with pleasant and moderate wine. Hear in silence, and for thy reverence good grace shall come to thee. Young man, scarcely speak in thy own cause. If thou be asked twice, let thy answer have a head.  

[Lectio3]
!Sir 32:12-17
Be among many as if thou wert unknown, and hear in silence, and at the same time seeking. In the midst of great men presume not, and where the elders are, speak not much. Before the hail goeth lightning: and before bashfulness goeth favour, and for thy reverence good grace shall come to thee. And at the hour of rising be not slack: but be first at home, and there take thy pastime, and there take thy thoughts, and not in offences and proud speech. And for all these things bless the Lord, that made thee, and that replenisheth thee with all His good things.
