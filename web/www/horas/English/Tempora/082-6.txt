[Lectio1]
Lesson from the book of Ecclesiastes
!Eccl 7:1-3
What need is there for a man to even seek things that are greater than himself, when he is ignorant of what is conducive for him in his life, [to make use of the] number of the days of his pilgrimage in the time that passes like a shadow? Or who can tell him what his future shall be afterwards under the sun? A good name is better to be had than precious ointments: and the day of death than the day of one’s birth. It is better to go to a house of mourning than to a house of feasting: for in that house is the end of all men warned about, and the living man thinketh on what is to come hereafter.

[Lectio2]
!Eccl 7:4-9
Anger is better than laughter: because through the sorrow of the countenance the soul of the offender [delinquent] is corrected. The heart of the wise is where there is sorrow [over sin], and the heart of the stupid is where there is mirth. It is better to be rebuked by a wise man, than to be deceived by the flattery of the stupid; for as the crackling sound of thorns burning under a pot, so is the laughter of stupid: now this laughter also is vanity. Calumnies trouble the wise man, and destroys the strength of his heart. Better is the end of [such] speech than the beginning of it. Better is the patient man [under this suffering] than the arrogant.

[Lectio3]
!Eccl 7:11-14
Say not: What thinkest thou is the cause that former times were better than they are now? For this manner of question is stupid. Getting Wisdom with wealth is more useful, and it availeth more to them that can still see the sun. For as wisdom is a protection, so money is a protection: but learning and wisdom have this advantage moreover [than money], that they give life to him that possesseth them. Consider the works of God, that no man can correct whom He hath despised.
