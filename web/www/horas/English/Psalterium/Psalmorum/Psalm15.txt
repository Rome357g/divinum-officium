15:1 Keep me, O Lord, for in Thee have I hoped. (2) I said unto the Lord: Thou art my Lord; for of my goods, no need hast Thou.
15:3 In the saints that are in His earth hath the Lord been wondrous; He hath wrought all His desires in them.
15:4a Their infirmities increased; thereupon they hastened. Diapsalm
15:4b I will not assemble their assemblies of blood, nor will I make remembrance of their name through my lips.
15:5 The Lord is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup. Thou art He that restorest mine inheritance unto me.
15:6 Portions have fallen to me that are among the best, for mine inheritance is most excellent to me.
15:7 I will bless the Lord Who hath given me understanding; moreover, even till night have my reins instructed me.
15:8 I beheld the Lord ever before me, for He is at my right hand, that I might not be shaken.
15:9 Therefore did my heart rejoice and my tongue was glad; moreover, my flesh shall dwell in hope.
15:10 For Thou wilt not abandon my soul in hades, nor wilt Thou suffer Thy Holy One to see corruption.
15:11 Thou hast made known to me the ways of life, Thou wilt fill me with gladness with Thy countenance; delights are in Thy right hand for ever.
