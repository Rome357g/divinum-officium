109:1a The Lord said unto my Lord: Sit Thou at My right hand,
109:1b until I make Thine enemies the footstool of Thy feet.
109:2 A sceptre of power shall the Lord send unto Thee out of Sion; rule Thou in the midst of Thine enemies.
109:3 With Thee is dominion in the day of Thy power, in the splendour of Thy saints. From the womb before the morning star have I begotten Thee.
109:4 The Lord hath sworn and will not repent: Thou art a priest for ever, after the order of Melchisedek.
109:5 The Lord at Thy right hand hath broken kings in the day of His wrath.
109:6 He shall judge among the nations, He shall fill them with dead bodies, He shall crush the heads of many upon the earth.
109:7 He shall drink of the brook in the way; therefore shall He lift up His head.
