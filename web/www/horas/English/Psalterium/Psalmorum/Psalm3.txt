3:1 A Psalm of David. When He Fled from the Face of Abessalom His Son, in the Wilderness.
3:2 O Lord, why are they multiplied that afflict me? Many rise up against me.
3:3 Many say unto my soul: There is no salvation for him in his God.
3:4 But Thou, O Lord, art my helper, my glory, and the lifter up of my head.
3:5 I cried unto the Lord with my voice, and He heard me out of His holy mountain.
3:6 I laid me down and slept; I awoke, for the Lord will help me.
3:7 I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people that set themselves against me round about.
3:8 Arise, O Lord, save me, O my God, for Thou hast smitten all who without cause are mine enemies; the teeth of sinners hast Thou broken.
3:9 Salvation is of the Lord, and Thy blessing is upon Thy people.
