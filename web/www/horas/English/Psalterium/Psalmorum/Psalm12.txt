12:1 How long, O Lord, wilt Thou utterly forget me? How long wilt Thou turn Thy face away from me?
12:2 How long shall I take counsel in my soul with grievings in my heart by day and by night?
12:3 How long shall mine enemy be exalted over me? (4a) Look upon me, hear me, O Lord my God;
12:4b enlighten mine eyes, lest at any time I sleep unto death. (5a) Lest at any time mine enemy say: I have prevailed against him.
12:5b They that afflict me will rejoice if I am shaken; (6a) but as for me, I have hoped in Thy mercy.
12:6b My heart will rejoice in Thy salvation. I will sing unto the Lord, Who is my benefactor, and I will chant unto the name of the Lord Most High.
