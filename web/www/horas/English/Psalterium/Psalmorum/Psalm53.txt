53:3 For the End: Among the Hymns of Instruction by David. When the Ziphites Came, and Said to Saul: Lo, is not David Hidden with Us? - O God, in Thy name save me, and in Thy strength do Thou judge me.
53:4 O God, hearken unto my prayer, give ear unto the words of my mouth.
53:5 For strangers are risen up against me, and mighty men have sought after my soul and have not set God before themselves.
53:6 For behold, God helpeth me, and the Lord is the protector of my soul.
53:7 He will bring evils upon mine enemies. Utterly destroy them by Thy truth.
53:8 Willingly shall I sacrifice unto Thee; I will confess Thy name, O Lord, for it is good.
53:9 For out of every affliction hast Thou delivered me, and mine eye hath looked down upon mine enemies.
