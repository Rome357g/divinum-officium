14:0 A Psalm of David.
14:1 O Lord, who shall abide in Thy tabernacle? and who shall dwell in Thy holy mountain?
14:2 He that walketh blameless and worketh righteousness,
14:3a Speaking truth in his heart, who hath not spoken deceitfully with his tongue,
14:3b Neither hath done evil to his neighbour, nor taken up a reproach against those near him.
14:4a In his sight he that worketh evil is set at nought, be he glorifieth them that fear the Lord.
14:4b He giveth oath to his neighbour, and forsweareth not. (5a) He hath not lent his money on usury, and hath not received bribes against the innocent.
14:5b He that doeth these things shall never be shaken.
