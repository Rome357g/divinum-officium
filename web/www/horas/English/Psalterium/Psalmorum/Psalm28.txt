28:0 A Psalm of David. A Processional of the Tabernacle.
28:1 Bring unto the Lord, ye sons of God, bring unto the Lord the sons of rams;
28:2 Bring unto the Lord glory and honour. Bring unto the Lord the glory due unto His name, worship the Lord in His holy court.
28:3 The voice of the Lord is upon the waters; the God of glory hath thundered, the Lord is upon the many waters.
28:4 The voice of the Lord in might, the voice of the Lord in majesty,
28:5 The voice of the Lord Who breaketh the cedars, yea, the Lord will break the cedars of Lebanon.
28:6 And He will break them small like the calf of Lebanon, and His beloved is like a son of the unicorns.
28:7 The voice of the Lord Who divideth the flame of fire, the voice of the Lord Who shaketh the wilderness, yea, the Lord will shake the wilderness of Kaddis.
28:9 The voice of the Lord gathereth the harts, and shall reveal the thickets of oak, and in His temple every man uttereth glory.
28:10 The Lord dwelleth in the flood, yea, the Lord shall sit as king for ever.
28:11 The Lord will give strength unto His people; the Lord will bless His people with peace.
