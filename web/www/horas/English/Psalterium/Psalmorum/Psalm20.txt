20:1 For the End: A Psalm of David.
20:2 O Lord, in Thy strength the king shall be glad, and in Thy salvation shall he rejoice exceedingly.
20:3 The desire of his heart hast Thou granted unto him, and hast not denied him the requests of his lips.
20:4 Thou wentest before him with the blessings of goodness, Thou hast set upon his head a crown of precious stone.
20:5 He asked life of Thee, and Thou gavest him length of days unto ages of ages.
20:6 Great is his glory in Thy salvation; glory and majesty shalt Thou lay upon him.
20:7 For Thou shalt give him blessing for ever and ever, Thou shalt gladden him in joy with Thy countenance.
20:8 For the king hopeth in the Lord, and through the mercy of the Most High shall he not be shaken.
20:9 Let Thy hand be found on all Thine enemies; let Thy right hand find all that hate Thee.
20:10 For Thou wilt make them as an oven of fire in the time of Thy presence; the Lord in His wrath will trouble them sorely and fire shall devour them.
20:11 Their fruit wilt Thou destroy from the earth, and their seed from the sons of men.
20:12 For they have intended evil against Thee, they have devised counsels which they shall not be able to establish.
20:13 For Thou shalt make them turn their backs; among those that are Thy remnant, Thou shalt make ready their countenance.
20:14 Be Thou exalted, O Lord, in Thy strength; we will sing and chant of Thy mighty acts.
