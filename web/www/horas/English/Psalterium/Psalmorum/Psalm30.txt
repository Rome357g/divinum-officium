30:1 For the End: A Psalm of David. In Time of Sudden Fear.
30:2 In Thee, O Lord, have I hoped, let me not be put to shame in the age to come; in Thy righteousness deliver me, and rescue me.
30:3 Bow down Thine ear unto me, make haste to rescue me,
30:3 Be Thou unto me a God to defend me and a house of refuge to save me.
30:4 For my strength and my refuge art Thou, and for Thy name's sake wilt Thou guide me and nourish me.
30:5 Thou wilt bring me out of this snare which they have hid for me, for Thou art my defender, O Lord.
30:6 Into Thy hands I will commit my spirit; Thou hast redeemed me, O Lord God of truth.
30:7 Thou hast hated them that cling to empty vanities;
30:7 But I have hoped in the Lord. I will rejoice and be glad in Thy mercy,
30:8 For Thou hast regarded my lowliness; Thou hast saved my soul out of necessities,
30:9 And hast not shut me up in the hands of enemies; Thou hast set my feet in a spacious place.
30:10 Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am afflicted; mine eye is troubled with anger, as also my soul and my belly.
30:11 For my life is spent with grief, and my years with groanings;
30:11 My strength hath grown weak in poverty, and my bones are troubled.
30:12 I am become a reproach among all mine enemies, and greatly for my neighbours also, and a fear to mine acquaintances.
30:12 They that saw me without fled from me. I am forgotten by the heart like a dead man.
30:13 I am become like a broken vessel. For I have heard the reproach of many that dwell round about;
30:14 When they assembled together against me, they devised to take away my life.
30:15 But as for me, I have hoped in Thee, O Lord; I said: Thou art my God; in Thy hands are my lots.
30:16 Deliver me from the hands of mine enemies, and from them that persecute me.
30:17 Make Thy face to shine upon Thy servant, save me in Thy mercy. O Lord, let me not be put to shame, for I have called upon Thee;
30:18 Let the ungodly be put to shame and let them be brought down into hades. Speechless be the deceitful lips
30:19 Which speak iniquity against the righteous man with arrogance and contempt.
30:20 How great is the multitude of Thy goodness, O Lord, which Thou hast hid for them that fear Thee,
30:20 Which Thou hast wrought for them that hope in Thee before the sons of men.
30:21 Thou shalt hide them in the secrecy of Thy presence from the disturbance of men;
30:21 Thou shalt shelter them in the tabernacle from contradiction of tongues.
30:22 Blessed is the Lord, for He hath made His mercy wondrous in a fortified city.
30:23 But as for me, I said in mine ecstasy: I am cast away from the presence of Thine eyes.
30:23 Therefore hast Thou heard the voice of my supplication when I cried unto Thee.
30:24 O love ye the Lord, all ye His saints, for the Lord requireth truth; and unto them that act with exceeding pride, He rendereth them their due.
30:25 Be ye manful, and let your heart be strengthened, all ye that hope in the Lord.
