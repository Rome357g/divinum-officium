18:1 For the End: A Psalm of David.
18:2 The heavens declare the glory of God, and the firmament proclaimeth the work of His hands.
18:3 Day unto day poureth forth speech, and night unto night proclaimeth knowledge.
18:4 There are no tongues nor words in which their voices are not heard.
18:5 Their sound hath gone forth into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world.
18:6a In the sun hath He set His tabernacle; and He, like a bridegroom coming forth from his chamber,
18:6b Will rejoice like a giant to run his course. (7a) From the outermost border of heaven is His going forth,
18:7b and His goal is unto the outermost part of heaven, and there shall no man hide himself from His heat.
18:8 The law of the Lord is blameless, converting souls; the testimony of the Lord is trustworthy, bringing wisdom to infants.
18:9 The statutes of the Lord are upright, gladdening the heart; the commandment of the Lord is far-shining, enlightening the eyes.
18:10 The fear of the Lord is pure, enduring for ever and ever; the judgements of the Lord are true, altogether justified,
18:11 More to be desired than gold and much precious stone, and sweeter than honey and the honeycomb.
18:12 Yea, for Thy servant keepeth them; in keeping them there is much reward.
18:13 As for transgressions, who will understand them? From my secret sins cleanse me, (14a) and from those of others spare Thy servant.
18:14b If they have not dominion over me, then blameless shall I be, and I shall be cleansed from great sin.
18:15a And the sayings of my mouth shall be unto Thy good pleasure, and the meditation of my heart shall be before Thee for ever,
18:15b O Lord, my helper and redeemer.
