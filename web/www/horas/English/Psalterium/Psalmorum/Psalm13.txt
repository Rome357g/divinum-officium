13:0 For the End: A Psalm of David.
13:1a The fool hath said in his heart: There is no God.
13:1b They are become corrupt and loathsome in their ways; there is none that doeth good, no not one.
13:2 The Lord looked down from heaven upon the sons of men, to see if there be any that understand or seek after God.
13:3a They are all gone astray, they are altogether useless, there is none that doeth good, no not one.
13:3b Their throat is an open sepulchre: with their tongues they have dealt deceitfully; the venom of asps is under their lips.
13:3c Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness: their feet are swift to shed blood.
13:3d Destruction and unhappiness are in their ways, and the way of peace they have not known: there is no fear of God before their eyes.
13:4 Shall not all they that work iniquity come to understanding? That eat up my people as they eat bread
13:5 They have not called upon the Lord. There have they trembled with fear where there is no fear;
13:6 For the Lord is in the generation of the righteous. The counsel of the poor man have ye shamed, but the Lord is his hope.
13:7 Who shall give out of Sion the salvation of Israel? When the Lord hath turned back the captivity of His people, Jacob shall rejoice and Israel shall be glad.
