113:1 When Israel went out of Egypt, * and the house of Jacob from among a barbarous people,
113:2 Judea became His sanctuary, * Israel his dominion.
113:3 The sea beheld and fled, * Jordan turned back.
113:4 The mountains skipped like rams, * and the hills like lambs of flocks.
113:5 What aileth thee, O sea, that thou fleddest? * And thou Jordan, that thou didst turn back?
113:6 Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams, * and ye hills like lambs of flocks?
113:7 At the presence of the Lord the earth was shaken, * at the presence of the God of Jacob,
113:8 Who turneth the rock into pools of waters, * and the precipice into fountains of waters.
113:9 Not unto us, O Lord, not unto us, * but unto Thy name give glory,
113:10 for Thy mercy and Thy truth, * Lest haply the heathen say: Where is their God?
113:11 But our God is in heaven and on earth; * all things soever He hath willed, He hath done.
113:12 The idols of the nations are of silver and gold, * the works of the hands of men.
113:13 They have a mouth, but shall not speak; * eyes have they, and shall not see.
113:14 Ears have they, and shall not hear; * noses have they, and shall not smell.
113:15 Hands they have, and shall not feel; † feet have they, and shall not walk; * they shall make no sound in their throat.
113:16 Let those that make them become like unto them, * and all that put their trust in them.
113:17 The house of Israel hath hoped in the Lord; * their helper and defender is He.
113:18 The house of Aaron hath hoped in the Lord; * their helper and defender is He.
113:19 They that fear the Lord have hoped in the Lord; * their helper and defender is He.
113:20a The Lord being mindful of us, * hath blessed us.
113:20b He hath blessed the house of Israel, * He hath blessed the house of Aaron.
113:21 He hath blessed them that fear the Lord, * the little with the great.
113:22 The Lord grant you increase, * to you and to your children.
113:23 Ye are blessed of the Lord, * Who made heaven and the earth.
113:24 The heaven of heaven belongeth to the Lord, * but the earth He gave unto the sons of men.
113:25 Not the dead shall praise Thee, O Lord, * nor any that go down to hades.
113:26 But we the living will bless the Lord * from henceforth and for evermore.
