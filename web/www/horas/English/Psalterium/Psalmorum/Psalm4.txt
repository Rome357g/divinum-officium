4:2a When I called upon Thee, O God of my righteousness, Thou didst hearken unto me; in mine affliction Thou hast enlarged me.
4:2b Have compassion on me and hear my prayer.
4:3 O ye sons of men, how long will ye be slow of heart? Why do ye love vanity, and seek after falsehood?
4:4 Know also that the Lord hath made wondrous His holy one; the Lord will hearken unto me when I cry unto Him.
4:5 Be angry, and sin not; feel compunction upon your beds for what ye say in your hearts.
4:6 Sacrifice a sacrifice of righteousness, and hope in the Lord. Many say: Who will show unto us good things?
4:7 The light of Thy countenance, O Lord, hath been signed upon us; Thou hast given gladness to my heart.
4:8 From the fruit of their wheat, wine, and oil are they multiplied.
4:9 In peace in the same place I shall lay me down and sleep.
4:10 For Thou, O Lord, alone hast made me to dwell in hope.
