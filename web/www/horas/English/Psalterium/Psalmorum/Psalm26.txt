26:0 The psalm of David before he was anointed.
26:1 The Lord is my light and my saviour; whom then shall I fear?
26:1 The Lord is the defender of my life; of whom then shall I be afraid?
26:2 When the wicked draw nigh against me to eat my flesh,
26:2 They that afflict me and are mine enemies, they themselves became weak and they fell.
26:3 Though a host should array itself against me, my heart shall not be afraid;
26:3 Though war should rise up against me, in this have I hoped.
26:4 One thing have I asked of the Lord, this will I seek after: That I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life,
26:4 That I may behold the delight of the Lord, and that I may visit His holy temple.
26:5 For He hid me in His tabernacle in the day of my troubles, He sheltered me in the secret place of His tabernacle,
26:6 Upon a rock hath He exalted me. And now, behold, He exalted my head above mine enemies.
26:6 I went round about and I sacrificed in His tabernacle a sacrifice of praise and jubilation; I will sing and I will chant unto the Lord.
26:7 Hearken, O Lord, unto my voice, wherewith I cried; have mercy on me, and hearken unto me.
26:8 My heart said unto Thee: I will seek the Lord. My face hath sought after Thee; Thy face, O Lord, will I seek.
26:9 Turn not Thy face from me and turn not away in wrath from Thy servant.
26:9 Be Thou my helper; cast me not utterly away, and forsake me not, O God my Saviour.
26:10 For my father and my mother have forsaken me, but the Lord hath taken me to Himself.
26:11 Set me a law, O Lord, in Thy way, and lead me in the right path because of mine enemies.
26:12 Deliver me not over unto the souls of them that afflict me, for unjust witnesses are risen up against me, and injustice hath lied to itself.
26:13 I believe that I shall see the good things of the Lord in the land of the living.
26:14 Wait on the Lord; be thou manful, and let thy heart be strengthened, and wait on the Lord.
