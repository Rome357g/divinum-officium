110:1 I will confess Thee, O Lord, with my whole heart, * in the council of the upright and in the congregation.
110:2 Great are the works of the Lord, * sought out in all the things that He hath willed.
110:3 Confession and majesty are His work, * and His righteousness abideth unto ages of ages.
110:4 He made a remembrance of His wondrous deeds; † merciful and compassionate is the Lord; * (5a) He hath given food to them that fear Him.
110:5b  He will be mindful for ever of His covenant; * (6) the power of His works hath He declared unto His people,
110:7 That He may give them the inheritance of the nations; * the works of His hands are truth and judgement.
110:8 Faithful are all His commandments, † confirmed unto ages of ages, * made in truth and uprightness.
110:9a He hath sent redemption unto His people, * He hath enjoined His covenant for ever;
110:9b (bow head) Holy and terrible is His name. * (10a) The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom;
110:10b and all they that foster this have a good understanding. * His praise abideth unto ages of ages.
