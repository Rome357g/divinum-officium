29:1 For the End: A Canticle Psalm at the Dedication of the House of David.
29:2 I will exalt Thee, O Lord, for Thou hast upheld me, and hast not made my foes to rejoice over me.
29:3 O Lord my God, I cried unto Thee, and Thou hast healed me.
29:4 O Lord, Thou hast brought up my soul out of hades; Thou hast saved me from them that go down into the pit.
29:5 Chant unto the Lord, O ye saints of His, and give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness.
29:6 For wrath is in His anger, but in His will there is life;
29:6 At evening shall weeping find lodging, but in the morning rejoicing.
29:7 And I said in my prosperity: I shall remain unshaken for ever.
29:8 O Lord, by Thy will hast Thou granted power to my beauty,
29:8 But Thou hast turned away Thy face, and I am become troubled.
29:9 Unto Thee, O Lord, will I cry, and unto my God will I make supplication.
29:10 What profit is there in my blood when I go down into corruption?
29:10 Shall dust confess Thee, or declare Thy truth?
29:11 The Lord hath heard me and hath had mercy on me; the Lord became my helper.
29:12 Thou hast turned my mourning into joy for me, Thou didst rend my sackcloth and didst gird me with gladness,
29:13 That my glory may chant unto Thee, and that I may not be pierced with sorrow; O Lord my God, I will confess Thee for ever.
