6:1 For the End: A Psalm of David among the Hymns for the Eighth.
6:2 O Lord, rebuke me not in Thine anger, nor chasten me in Thy wrath.
6:3 Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am weak. Heal me, O Lord, for my bones are troubled,
6:4 And my soul is troubled greatly; but Thou, O Lord, how long?
6:5 Turn to me again, O Lord, deliver my soul; save me for Thy mercy's sake.
6:6 For in death there is none that is mindful of Thee, and in hades who will confess Thee?
6:7 I toiled in my groaning; every night I will wash my bed, with tears will I water my couch.
6:8 Through wrath is mine eye become troubled, I have grown old among all mine enemies.
6:9 Depart from me all ye that work vanity, for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping.
6:10 The Lord hath heard my supplication, the Lord hath received my prayer.
6:11 Let all mine enemies be greatly put to shame and be troubled, let them be turned back, and speedily be greatly put to shame.
