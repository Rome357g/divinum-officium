22:0 A Psalm of David.
22:1 The Lord ruleth me, and I shall not want. In a place of green pasture,
22:2 There hath He made me to dwell; beside the water of rest hath He nurtured me. He hath converted my soul,
22:3 He hath led me on the paths of righteousness for His name's sake.
22:4 For though I should walk in the midst of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for Thou art with me;
22:4 Thy rod and Thy staff, they have comforted me.
22:5 Thou hast prepared a table for me in the presence of them that afflict me.
22:5 Thou hast anointed my head with oil, and Thy chalice which filleth me, how excellent it is!
22:6 And Thy mercy shall pursue me all the days of my life,
22:6 And I will dwell in the house of the Lord unto length of days.
