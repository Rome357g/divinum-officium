2:1 Why have the heathen raged, and the peoples meditated empty things?
2:2 The kings of the earth were aroused, and the rulers were assembled together, against the Lord, and against His Christ
2:3 Let us break their bonds asunder, and let us cast away their yoke from us.
2:4 He that dwelleth in the heavens shall laugh them to scorn, and the Lord shall deride them.
2:5 Then shall He speak unto them in His wrath, and in His anger shall He trouble them.
2:6 But as for Me, I was established as king by Him, upon Sion His holy mountain, proclaiming the commandment of the Lord.
2:7 The Lord said unto Me: Thou art My Son, this day have I begotten Thee.
2:8 Ask of Me, and I will give Thee the nations for Thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for Thy possession.
2:9 Thou shalt herd them with a rod of iron; Thou shalt shatter them like a potter's vessels.
2:10 And now, O ye kings, understand; be instructed, all ye that judge the earth.
2:11 Serve ye the Lord with fear, and rejoice in Him with trembling.
2:12 Lay hold of instruction, lest at any time the Lord be angry, and ye perish from the righteous way.
2:13 When quickly His wrath be kindled, blessed are all that have put their trust in Him.
