16:0 A Prayer of David.
16:1a Hearken, O Lord, unto my righteousness, attend unto my supplication.
16:1b Give ear unto my prayer, which cometh not from deceitful lips.
16:2 From before Thy face let my judgement come forth, let mine eyes behold uprightness.
16:3 Thou hast proved my heart, Thou hast visited it in the night, Thou hast tried me by fire, and unrighteousness was not found in me.
16:4 That my mouth might not speak of the works of men, for the sake of the words of Thy lips have I kept the ways that are hard.
16:5 Set my footsteps in Thy paths, that my steps may not be shaken.
16:6 I have cried for Thou hast hearkened unto me, O God. Incline Thine ear unto me, and hearken unto my words.
16:7 Let Thy mercies be made wonderful, O Thou that savest them that hope in Thee.
16:8a From them that have resisted Thy right hand, keep me, O Lord, as the apple of Thine eye.
16:8b In the shelter of Thy wings wilt Thou shelter me, (9a) from the face of the ungodly which have oppressed me.
16:9b Mine enemies have surrounded my soul, (10) they have enclosed themselves with their own fat, their mouth hath spoken pride.
16:11 They that cast me out have now encircled me, they have set their eyes to look askance on the earth.
16:12 They have taken me as might a lion ready for his prey, and as might a lion's whelp that dwelleth in hiding.
16:13 Arise, O Lord, overtake them and trip their heels; deliver my soul from ungodly men, Thy sword (14a) from the enemies of Thy hand.
16:14b O Lord, from Thy few do Thou separate them from the earth in their life; yea, with Thy hidden treasures hath their belly been filled.
16:14c They have satisfied themselves with swine and have left the remnants to their babes.
16:15 But as for me, in righteousness shall I appear before Thy face; I shall be filled when Thy glory is made manifest to me.
