11:2 Save me, O Lord, for a righteous man there is no more; for truths have diminished from the sons of men.
11:3 Vain things hath each man spoken to his neighbour; deceitful lips are in his heart, and in his heart hath he spoken evils.
11:4 Let the Lord destroy all deceitful lips and the tongue that speaketh boastful words,
11:5 which have said: Our tongue will we magnify, our lips are our own. Who is lord over us?
11:6a Because of the distress of the beggars and the groaning of the poor, now will I arise, saith the Lord;
11:6b I will establish them in salvation, I will be manifest therein.
11:7 The words of the Lord are pure words, silver that is fired, tried in the earth, brought to sevenfold purity.
11:8 Thou, O Lord, shalt keep us and shalt preserve us from this generation, and for evermore.
11:9 The ungodly walk round about; to the measure of Thy loftiness hast Thou esteemed the sons of men.
