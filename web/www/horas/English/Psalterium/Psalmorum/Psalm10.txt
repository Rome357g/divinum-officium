10:1 For the End: A Psalm of David.
10:2 In the Lord have I hoped; how will ye say to my soul: Flee unto the mountains like a sparrow?
10:3 For behold, the sinners have bent their bow, they have prepared arrows for the quiver, to shoot down in a moonless night the upright of heart.
10:4 For what Thou hast formed they have destroyed; and the righteous man, what hath he done?
10:5a The Lord is in His holy temple; the Lord, in heaven is His throne;
10:5b His eyes are set upon the poor man, His eyelids examine the sons of men.
10:6 The Lord examineth the righteous man and the ungodly; but he that loveth unrighteousness hateth his own soul.
10:7 He will rain down snares upon sinners; fire and brimstone and wind of tempest shall be the portion of their chalice.
10:8 For the Lord is righteous and hath loved righteousness; upon uprightness hath His countenance looked.
