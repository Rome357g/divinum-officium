5:1 For the End: A Psalm of David for Her That Obtained the Inheritance.
5:2 Unto my words, give ear, O Lord; hear my cry.
5:3 Attend unto the voice of my supplication, O my King and my God;
5:4 For unto Thee will I pray, O Lord. In the morning Thou shalt hear my voice.
5:5 In the morning shall I stand before Thee, and Thou shalt look upon me; for not a God that willest iniquity art Thou.
5:6 He that worketh evil shall not dwell near Thee, nor shall transgressors abide before Thine eyes.
5:7a Thou hast hated all them that work iniquity; Thou shalt destroy all them that speak a lie.
5:7b A man that is bloody and deceitful shall the Lord abhor. (8a) But as for me, in the multitude of Thy mercy,
5:8b Shall I go into Thy house; I shall worship toward Thy holy temple in fear of Thee.
5:9 O Lord, guide me in the way of Thy righteousness; because of mine enemies, make straight my way before Thee.
5:10  For in their mouth there is no truth; their heart is vain.
5:11a Their throat is an open sepulchre, with their tongues have they spoken deceitfully; judge them, O God.
5:11b Let them fall down on account of their own devisings; according to the multitude of their ungodliness, cast them out, for they have embittered Thee, O Lord.
5:12a And let all them be glad that hope in Thee; they shall ever rejoice, and Thou shalt dwell among them.
5:12b And all shall glory in Thee that love Thy name, (13a) for Thou shalt bless the righteous.
5:13b O Lord, as with a shield of Thy good pleasure hast Thou crowned us.
