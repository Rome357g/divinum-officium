25:0 David's.
25:1 Judge me, O Lord, for in mine innocence have I walked; and hoping in the Lord I shall not grow weak.
25:2 Prove me, O Lord, and try me; prove with fire my reins and my heart.
25:3 For Thy mercy is before mine eyes, and I have been well-pleasing in Thy truth.
25:4 I have not set with the council of vanity, nor shall I go in with them that transgress the law.
25:5 I have hated the congregation of evil-doers, and with the ungodly will I not sit.
25:6 I will wash my hands in innocency and I will compass Thine altar, O Lord,
25:7 That I may hear the voice of Thy praise and tell of all Thy wondrous works.
25:8 O Lord, I have loved the beauty of Thy house, and the place where Thy glory dwelleth.
25:9 Destroy not my soul with the ungodly, nor my life with men of blood,
25:10 In whose hands are iniquities; their right hand is full of bribes.
25:11 But as for me, in mine innocence have I walked; redeem me, O Lord, and have mercy on me.
25:12 My foot hath stood in uprightness; in the churches will I bless Thee, O Lord.
