54:2 For the End: Among the Hymns of Instruction by David. Give ear, O God, unto my prayer, and disdain not my supplication; attend unto me, and hear me.
54:3 I was grieved in my meditation, and I was troubled at the voice of the enemy and at the oppression of the sinner;
54:4 Because they have turned iniquity upon me, and with wrath were they angry against me.
54:5 My heart is troubled within me, and the terror of death is fallen upon me.
54:6 Fear and trembling are come upon me, and darkness hath covered me.
54:7 And I said: Who will give me wings like a dove? And I will fly, and be at rest.
54:8 Lo, I have fled afar off and have dwelt in the wilderness.
54:9 I waited for God that saveth me from faintheartedness and from tempest.
54:10 Plunge them into the depths, O Lord, and divide their tongues, for I have seen iniquity and gainsaying in the city.
54:11 Day and night they go round about her upon her walls; iniquity and toil and unrighteousness are in the midst of her.
54:12 And usury and deceit have not departed from her streets.
54:13 For if mine enemy had reviled me, I might have endured it.
54:13 And if he that hateth me had spoken boastful words against me, I might have hid myself from him.
54:14 But thou it was, O man of like soul with me, my guide and my familiar friend,
54:15 Thou who together with me didst sweeten my repasts; in the house of God I walked with thee in oneness of mind.
54:16 Let death come upon such ones, and let them go down alive into hades.
54:16 For wickedness is in their dwellings, and in the midst of them.
54:17 As for me, unto God have I cried, and the Lord hearkened unto me.
54:18 Evening, morning, and noonday will I tell of it and will declare it, and He will hear my voice.
54:19 He will redeem my soul in peace from them that draw nigh unto me, for they among many were with me.
54:20 God will hear, and He will humble them, He that is before the ages.
54:20 For to them there is no requital, because they have not feared God; He hath stretched forth His hand in retribution.
54:21 They have defiled His covenant; they were scattered by the wrath of His countenance, and their hearts have convened.
54:22 Their words were smoother than oil, and yet they are darts.
54:23 Cast thy care upon the Lord, and He will nourish thee; He will never permit the righteous to be shaken.
54:24 But Thou, O God, shalt bring those men down into the pit of destruction.
54:24 Bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half their days; but as for me, O Lord, I will hope in Thee.
