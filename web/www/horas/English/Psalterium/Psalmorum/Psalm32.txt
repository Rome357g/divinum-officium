32:1 Rejoice in the Lord, O ye righteous; praise is meet for the upright.
32:2 Give praise to the Lord with the harp, chant unto Him with the ten-stringed psaltery.
32:3 Sing unto Him a new song, chant well unto Him with jubilation.
32:4 For the word of the Lord is true, and all His works are in faithfulness.
32:5 The Lord loveth mercy and judgement; the earth is full of the mercy of the Lord.
32:6 By the Word of the Lord were the heavens established, and all the might of them by the Spirit of His mouth,
32:7 Who gathereth together as into a wineskin the waters of the sea, Who layeth up the abysses in storehouses.
32:8 Let all the earth fear the Lord, and let all the inhabitants of the world be shaken before Him.
32:9 For he spake, and they came into being; He commanded, and they were created.
32:10 The Lord scattereth the plans of the heathens, He setteth aside the devices of the peoples, and He bringeth to nought the plans of princes.
32:11 But the counsel of the Lord abideth unto eternity, the thoughts of His heart unto generation and generation.
32:12 Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord, the people whom He hath chosen for His inheritance.
32:13 The Lord looked down from heaven, He beheld all the sons of men.
32:14 From His habitation which He prepared, He looked upon all the inhabitants of the earth,
32:15 He that alone fashioned the heart of them, Who understandeth all their works.
32:16 A king is not saved by great might, nor shall a giant be saved by the magnitude of his own strength.
32:17 Futile is the horse for salvation, nor by the magnitude of his might shall he be saved.
32:18 Behold, the eyes of the Lord are upon them that fear Him, upon them that hope in His mercy.
32:19 To deliver their souls from death, and to nourish them in famine.
32:20 Our soul shall wait for the Lord, for he is our helper and our defender.
32:21 For our heart shall be glad in Him, and in His holy name have we hoped.
32:22 Let Thy mercy, O Lord, be upon us, according as we have hoped in Thee.
