23:0 A Psalm of David, on the First Day of the Week.
23:1 The earth is the Lord's, and the fullness thereof, the world, and all that dwell therein.
23:2 He hath founded it upon the seas, and upon the rivers hath He prepared it.
23:3 Who shall ascend into the mountain of the Lord? Or who shall stand in His holy place?
23:4 He that is innocent in hands and pure in heart, who hath not received his soul in vain, and hath not sworn deceitfully to his neighbour.
23:5 Such a one shall receive a blessing from the Lord, and mercy from God his Saviour.
23:6 This is the generation of them that seek the Lord, of them that seek the face of the God of Jacob.
23:7 Lift up your gates, O ye princes; and be ye lifted up, ye everlasting gates, and the King of Glory shall enter in.
23:8 Who is this King of Glory? The Lord strong and mighty, the Lord, mighty in war.
23:9 Lift up your gates, O ye princes; and be ye lifted up, ye everlasting gates, and the King of Glory shall enter in.
23:10 Who is this King of Glory? The Lord of hosts, He is the King of Glory.
