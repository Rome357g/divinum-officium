27:0 A Psalm of David
27:1 Unto Thee, O Lord, will I cry; O my God, be not silent unto me, lest, if Thou be silent to me, I become like them that go down into the pit.
27:2 Hearken, O Lord, unto my supplication when I pray unto Thee, when I lift up my hands toward Thy holy temple.
27:3 Draw me not in with sinners, and with the workers of unrighteousness destroy me not,
27:3 Who speak peace with their neighbours, but evils are in their hearts.
27:4 Give unto them, O Lord, according to their deeds, and according to the wickedness of their endeavours;
27:4 According to the work of their hands, give unto them. Render their reward unto them.
27:5 For they have not understood the works of the Lord, and the works of His hands. Thou shalt destroy them, and shalt not build them up.
27:6 Blessed is the Lord, because He hath heard the voice of my supplication.
27:7 The Lord is my helper and my defender; my heart hath hoped in Him, and I am helped
27:7 And my flesh hath flourished again, and out of my desire will I confess Him.
27:8 The Lord is the strength of His people, and the champion of salvation for His anointed one.
27:9 Save Thy people and bless Thine inheritance; shepherd them and bear them up unto eternity.
