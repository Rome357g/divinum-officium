21:1 For the End: A Psalm of David concerning Help That Cometh in the Morning.
21:2 O God, my God, attend to me; why hast Thou forsaken me? Far from my salvation are the words of my transgressions.
21:3 My God, I will cry by day, and wilt Thou not hearken? and by night, and it shall not be unto folly for me.
21:4 But as for Thee, Thou dwellest in the sanctuary, O Praise of Israel.
21:5 In Thee have our fathers hoped; they hoped, and Thou didst deliver them.
21:6 Unto Thee they cried, and were saved; in Thee they hoped, and were not brought to shame.
21:7 But as for me, I am a worm, and not a man, a reproach of men, and the outcast of the people.
21:8 All that look upon me have laughed me to scorn; they have spoken with their lips and have wagged their heads:
21:9 He hoped in the Lord; let Him deliver him, let Him save him, for He desireth him.
21:10 For Thou art He that drewest me forth from the womb; my hope from the breasts of my mother.
21:11O On Thee was I cast from the womb; from my mother's womb, Thou art my God. Depart not from me,
21:12 For tribulation is nigh, for there is none to help me.
21:13 Many bullocks have encircled me, fat bulls have surrounded me.
21:14 They have opened their mouth against me, as might a lion ravenous and roaring.
21:15 I have been poured out like water, and scattered are all my bones;
21:15 My heart is become like wax melting in the midst of my bowels.
21:16 My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue hath cleaved to my throat, and into the dust of death hast Thou brought me down.
21:17 For many dogs have encircled me, the congregation of evil doers hath surrounded me;
21:17 They have pierced my hands and my feet. They have numbered all my bones,
21:18 And they themselves have looked and stared upon me. They have parted my garments amongst themselves, and for my vesture have they cast lots.
21:20 But Thou, O Lord, remove not Thy help far from me; attend unto mine aid.
21:21 Rescue my soul from the sword, even this only-begotten one of mine from the hand of the dog.
21:22 Save me from the mouth of the lion, and my lowliness from the horns of the unicorns.
21:23 I will declare Thy name unto my brethren, in the midst of the church will I hymn Thee.
21:24 Ye that fear the Lord, praise Him; all ye that are of the seed of Jacob, glorify Him;
21:25 Let all fear Him that are of the seed of Israel. For He hath not set at naught nor abhorred the supplications of the pauper,
21:25 Nor hath He turned His face from me; and when I cried unto Him, He hearkened unto me.
21:26 From Thee is my praise; in the great church will I confess Thee; my vows will I pay before them that fear Thee.
21:27 The poor shall eat and be filled, and they that seek the Lord shall praise Him; their hearts shall live for ever and ever.
21:28 All the ends of the earth shall remember and shall turn unto the Lord,
21:28 and all the kindreds of the Gentiles shall worship before Him.
21:29 For the kingdom is the Lord's and He Himself is sovereign of the gentiles.
21:30 All they that be fat upon the earth have eaten and worshipped; all they that go down into the earth shall fall down before Him.
21:31 Yea, my soul liveth for Him, and my seed shall serve Him.
21:32 The generation that cometh shall be told of the Lord, and they shall proclaim His righteousness to a people that shall be born, which the Lord hath made.
21:29 For the kingdom is the Lord’s; * and he shall have dominion over the nations.
21:30 All the fat ones of the earth have eaten and have adored: * all they that go down to the earth shall fall before him.
21:31 And to him my soul shall live: * and my seed shall serve him.
21:32 There shall be declared to the Lord a generation to come: * and the heavens shall shew forth his justice to a people that shall be born, which the Lord hath made.
