Saint Agapitus, Martyr

[Oratio]
Let Thy Church rejoice, O God, trusting in the suffrages of blessed Agapitus Thy Martyr: and by his glorious prayers, may she both remain devout and stand secure.

!Commemoration of Saint Agapitus, Martyr
Agapitus of Palestrina, fifteen years of age, in the time of the emperor Aurelian, most desirous of martyrdom, when for the constancy of his religion, by command of the emperor, he had first been beaten for a very long time with sinews, and then cast into a foul prison, so that for four days he tasted nothing at all, was brought forth from custody. And when burning coals had been placed upon his head, giving thanks unto God, he was again afflicted with stripes, and was suspended naked by his feet in such wise that a great smoke from the fire beneath overwhelmed his face. Then boiling water was poured into his belly, and his jaws were broken. At which time the judge, having fallen from the tribunal, died a little while after. The emperor being enraged at this matter, and commanding the holy youth to be thrown to the beasts, when they dared not touch him, he was struck with the sword at Palestrina.
