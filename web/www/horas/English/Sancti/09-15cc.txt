[Officium]
Saint Nicomedes the Martyr

[Oratio]
Be present, O Lord, to Thy people: that, receiving the illustrious merits of blessed Nicomedes Thy Martyr, it may always be helped by his patronage in obtaining Thy mercy.
$Per Dominum

[Lectio93]
Nicomedes the priest, in the persecution of the Christians under the emperor Domitian, because he had buried the body of the virgin Felicula, who had been slain by the count Flaccus for the confession of the Christian faith, was ordered to be apprehended; and being led to the statues of the gods, when he had constantly refused that he was willing to sacrifice to them (which he was commanded to do), for the reason that sacrifice is due to the one true God who reigns in heaven, he was beaten with leaded scourges, and in that martyrdom rendered up his soul to God. Whose body, when the same count had ordered it to be thrown into the flowing Tiber, Justus, a cleric of Nicomedes, having diligently sought it out, buried it honourably in a sepulchre near the walls of the City on the Nomentan Way.
