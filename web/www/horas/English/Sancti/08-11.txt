[Officium]
Saints Tiburtius and Susanna, Virgin, Martyrs

[Oratio]
May the continued protections of Thy holy Martyrs Tiburtius and Susanna foster us, O Lord: for Thou ceasest not propitiously to regard those whom Thou hast granted to be helped by such aids.
$Per Dominum

[Lectio93]
Tiburtius, son of Chromatius the prefect of the City, made a Christian by the work of Saint Sebastian, when for that cause he had been brought before the judge Fabian and was preaching much before him concerning the faith of Christ, the judge, growing hot with anger, ordered the pavement to be strewn with glowing coals. Presently he saith: Tiburtius, either it is necessary that thou sacrifice to our gods, or thou must walk with bare feet over these coals. But he, fortifying himself with the sign of the cross and walking confidently upon the live coals, saith: Learn from this that He alone is God whom the Christians worship; for the live coals seem to me as flowers. Which when (in the year 286) it was attributed to magical arts, Tiburtius was led outside the City, and on the Lavican Way at the third milestone from the City was struck with the sword, and there is buried by the Christians. On which day Susanna, a most noble virgin, because she refused the marriage of Galerius Maximian, son of the emperor Diocletian, as one who had vowed her virginity to God; after many kinds of torments by which the holy purpose of the Virgin was variously tried, in her own house, by the command of the emperor, was struck with the sword, and passed into heaven unto the double reward of virginity and of martyrdom.
