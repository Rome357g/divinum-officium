[Officium]
Saint Peter Claver, Confessor

[Oratio]
O God, who, about to call the Negroes carried off into slavery unto the knowledge of Thy name, didst strengthen blessed Peter with marvellous charity and patience in helping them: grant us by his intercession; that, seeking the things that are of Jesus Christ, we may love our neighbours in deed and in truth.
$Per eumdem

[Lectio94]
Peter Claver, a Spaniard born at Verdu, joined the Society of Jesus as a youth of most innocent morals. In the largest of the Balearic Islands, he was instructed concerning the labors prepared for him by Saint Alphonsus Rodriguez, who had divinely received many revelations concerning his future apostolate. Having been raised to the priesthood at Cartagena in South America, he completely devoted his life, under the pledge of a solemn vow, to the Negroes brought thither by a shameful trade. Moreover, in cherishing, nourishing, caring for, instructing in Christian doctrine, and baptizing them, it can scarcely be told with how great patience and charity he labored for more than forty years. Very many who were despaired of by physicians he suddenly restored to health, to many deprived of the light of their eyes he restored sight, and three he raised from the dead. Preeminent in these merits and heroic virtues, he had completed seventy-four years when, on the birthday of the Mother of God, whom he had cherished with the tenderest affection, he passed to the heavenly citizens. Leo the Thirteenth enrolled him in the catalog of the holy Saints and constituted him the special heavenly patron of the sacred missions to the Negroes.
