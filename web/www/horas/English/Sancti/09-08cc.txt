[Officium]
Saint Hadrian, Martyr

[Name]
Adrian

[Lectio93]
Hadrian, while persecuting Christians at Nicomedia by order of the emperor Maximian, having often admired their constancy in the confession of the faith and in the endurance of torments, was strongly moved by that matter and converted himself unto Christ. For which reason he was cast into prison together with twenty-three other Christians; where Natalia his wife, who herself had already believed in Christ, visiting him, inflamed him unto martyrdom. And so, being brought forth from custody, he was scourged with rods for so long a time until his bowels flowed forth. At last, his legs being broken and his hands and feet cut off, together with many others he happily completed the contest of martyrdom.
