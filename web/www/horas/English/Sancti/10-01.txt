[Officium]
Saint Remigius, Bishop and Confessor

[Name]
Remigius

[Lectio93]
Remigius, bishop of Rheims, flourished under Clovis king of the Franks; whom he also baptized, and, first of all, by doctrine and miracles led the Franks to the faith of Christ the Lord. By his prayer a dead girl came to life again. He interpreted many books of the divine Scripture. For more than seventy years he administered the church of Rheims with the highest praise. Many miracles that followed approved the holiness of his life and death.
