Saints Protus and Hyacinth, Martyrs

[Oratio]
May the precious confession of Thy blessed Martyrs Protus and Hyacinth, O Lord, foster us: and may their pious intercession continually protect us.

!Commemoration for the holy Martyrs Protus and Hyacinth
Protus and Hyacinth, brothers, eunuchs of the blessed Virgin Eugenia, baptized together with her by Bishop Helenus, and devoted to the study of the divine Scriptures, lived for some time in Egypt among the ascetics with marvellous humility and holiness of life. But afterwards, having followed the holy Virgin Eugenia to Rome, under the emperor Gallienus they were apprehended in the City on account of the profession of the Christian faith. Since in no way could it be obtained of them that, abandoning the Christian religion, they should worship the gods, they were beaten with severe stripes and struck with the axe on the third of the Ides of September. [11th day of September].
