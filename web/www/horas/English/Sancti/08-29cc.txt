[Officium]
Saint Sabina, Martyr

[Name]
Sabina

[Lectio93]
Sabina, a Roman woman, wife of Valentinus a most illustrious man, instructed in the precepts of the Christian faith by the virgin Seraphia, after the martyrdom of the holy Virgin, gathered her relics and buried them with pious funeral rites. For which cause a little later, in the reign of the emperor Hadrian, she was apprehended and brought before the judge Elpidius. To whom he said: Art thou that Sabina most noble both by birth and by marriage? But she answered: I am: yet I give thanks unto my Lord Jesus Christ, who through the intercession of His handmaid Seraphia hath delivered me from the power of the demons. Whom, when he had variously tempted that she might change her purpose, and could not move her from the constancy of the faith, the prefect, having pronounced sentence that she contemned the gods, condemned her to death. Her body was laid by the Christians in the same sepulchre in which she herself had placed Seraphia, the instructress of her faith.
