Saint Zephyrinus, Pope and Martyr

[Name]
Zephyrinus

[Oratio] (nisi communi Summorum Pontificum)
Grant, we beseech Thee, almighty God, that we may be instructed by the examples of blessed Zephyrinus, Thy Martyr and Pontiff, in whose merits we rejoice.
$Per Dominum

[Lectio93]
Zephyrinus, a Roman, having been taken up to govern the Church in the time of the Emperor Severus, (in the year 202) ordained that those who were to be ordained should, at a fitting time and in the presence of many of the clergy and laity, be initiated into the sacred rites according to custom; and that men of learning and of approved life should be chosen for that office. He further decreed that when a bishop was celebrating the Divine mysteries all the priests should assist. He likewise instituted that a patriarch, a primate, or a metropolitan should not pass sentence against a bishop unless supported by the Apostolic Authority [The Holy See]. He lived in the pontificate eighteen years and eighteen days. He held four ordinations in the month of December, in which he created thirteen priests, seven deacons, and thirteen bishops for diverse places. Under the Emperor Antoninus he was crowned with martyrdom and was buried on the Appian Way near the cemetery of Callistus, on the seventh of the Kalends of September.
