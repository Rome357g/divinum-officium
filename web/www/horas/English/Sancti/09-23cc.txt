[Officium]
Saint Thecla, Virgin and Martyr

[Name]
Thecla

[Oratio]
Grant, we beseech Thee, almighty God: that we who keep the birthday of Thy blessed Virgin and Martyr Thecla may both rejoice in the annual solemnity and profit by the example of so great a faith.
$Per Dominum

[Lectio93]
Thecla the virgin, born at Iconium of illustrious parents, instructed by the Apostle Paul in the precepts of the faith, is celebrated with the wonderful praises of the holy Fathers. Who, being in her eighteenth year, having left her betrothed Thamiris, when her parents had accused her because she was a Christian, first armed with the sign of the cross, cast herself into a burning pile which was prepared for her unless she renounced Christ. But the fire being extinguished by a rain which suddenly arose, she came to Antioch; where, having been exposed to wild beasts and bound to bulls driven in different directions, soon afterwards cast into a pit full of serpents, she is delivered from all by the grace of Jesus Christ. By the ardour of whose faith and the sanctity of whose life many were converted to Christ. Returning again to her country, she withdrew alone into a mountain. Afterwards, distinguished by many virtues and miracles, in her ninetieth year she departed to the Lord, and was buried at Seleucia.
