!Commemoration of Saint Gorgonius, Martyr
Gorgonius, born at Nicomedia, chamberlain of the emperor Diocletian, with the help of his colleague Dorotheus, brought all the other ministers of the chamber unto the faith of Christ. But both of them, when on a certain day they had seen a Martyr most bitterly tortured before Diocletian, were inflamed by his example with the love of martyrdom. And so this cry broke forth from both of them: Why, O Emperor, dost thou punish this man only, by condemning an opinion which we share with him? His belief is our belief. Our will is the same. Them therefore the emperor ordered to be bound and cut to pieces with scourges, so that the skin was broken over the whole body, and vinegar mixed with salt to be poured into the wounds; presently he commanded them, being bound upon a gridiron, to have the force of glowing coals placed under them. At length, variously tortured, they were put to death by hanging. And the body of holy Gorgonius was at some time brought to Rome, buried between the two Laurels on the Latin Way, and afterwards translated by the supreme Pontiff Gregory the Fourth into the basilica of the Prince of the Apostles.
