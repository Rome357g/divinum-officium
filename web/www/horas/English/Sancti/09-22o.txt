Saints Maurice and Companions, Martyrs

[Oratio]
Grant, we beseech Thee, almighty God: that the festive solemnity of Thy holy Martyrs Maurice and his Companions may make us joyful; that, relying on their suffrages, we may glory in their birthday.
$Per Dominum

[Lectio93]
When the emperor Maximian, having led an army into Gaul, had halted in the territory of the Seduni for the sake of sacrifice, the Theban legion, lest it should contaminate itself by association with the impious rites, withdrew from the rest of the forces. Wherefore the emperor sent soldiers to them, who in his name should announce that, if they wished to be safe, they should return to the camp to the sacrifices: who answered that they were hindered by the Christian religion. He, taking their answer most indignantly, burned with greater anger than before. Therefore, having sent a part of the army against the Thebans, he first ordered every tenth man of them to be killed; which martyrdom they preferred of their own accord, and especially at the exhortation of Maurice, to endure rather than to do the commands of the wicked emperor. And then he orders all the rest, most constantly preaching Christ, to be slaughtered by the whole army on the tenth of the Calends of October.
